# ADR-0008 — Competition Must Not Reward Reckless Speed

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

Gamification can motivate engagement but could incentivize dangerous public-road behavior if maximum speed/high-G extremes become objectives.

## Decision

Rankings focus on smoothness, consistency, control, improvement, clean-drive streaks, and other defensible categories. Maximum speed remains private trip telemetry and is not a primary competitive score.

## Consequences

Positive:
- safer product incentives;
- differentiates product from racing apps;
- better social responsibility.

Negative:
- some users seeking speed competition may be less interested.
